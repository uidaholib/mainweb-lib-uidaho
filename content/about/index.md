The University of Idaho Library has grown from a single classroom located in the University Administration Building in 1892, to become <strong>the largest library in the state of Idaho</strong>, housing well over a million books and subscribing to almost ten thousand periodicals, in print and online. The library has also served for over a century as <a href="{{ '/find/govdocs/' | relative_url }}">an official regional depository of U.S. federal government publications</a>, making almost two million government documents available to the public. The library's <a href="{{ '/special-collections/' | relative_url }}">Special Collections</a> are an invaluable resource for researchers, providing access to historical photographs, state documents, university historical materials, rare books, <a href="https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/digital/">digital collections</a>, and <a href="http://www.ijc.uidaho.edu/">the International Jazz Collections</a>, the premier jazz archives of the Pacific Northwest.
