## Instructor guidance

- [U of I AI Resources and Guidance](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/sites/InsideUI-AIatUIdaho/SitePages/Home.aspx) - U of I's campus-wide guidance, including [AI in the Classroom](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/sites/InsideUI-AIatUIdaho/SitePages/AI-in-the-Classroom.aspx) (U of I login required)
- [U of I CETL AI Academy](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/sites/InsideUI-CETL/SitePages/AI-Academy.aspx) - CETL's guidance on AI and syllabus language, as well as AI video recordings and recommended resources; U of I's AI Literacy Framework is included, with learning objectives and core AI literacy competencies (U of I login required)
- [AI Literacy in the Age of Generative AI](https://libguides.library.arizona.edu/ai-literacy-instructors) - University of Arizona's instructor guide, with a [student companion guide](https://libguides.library.arizona.edu/students-chatgpt/)
- [Critical AI for Educators](https://padlet.com/kathrynconrad/critical-ai-literacy-for-educators-umh8r80mykrw0d68) - resources curated by Sean Kamperman and Kathryn Conrad, University of Kansas

## AI basics: tutorials and videos for students

- [AI Basics]({{ '/datahub/ai-basics.html' | relative_url }}) - U of I Library's introduction to AI
- [Generative AI tutorials](https://libguides.library.arizona.edu/genAI/tutorials) - from the University of Arizona Libraries
- [How Large Language Models Actually Work](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPA3GFqdHv_rzpz99mtKO6MRe6BYyEiPH) - short, informal video reels from Casey Fiesler of CU Boulder
- [Generative AI (Code.org)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLzdnOPI1iJNfJLo5nNGPHo0xCd9Xw5lSs) - very basic introductory videos by code.org (now Code AI)

## Additional AI guides

- [Miami University Libraries' Guide to AI](https://libguides.lib.miamioh.edu/ai/home) - includes information on citations and agentic AI
- [University of Arizona Libraries' Generative AI Guide](https://libguides.library.arizona.edu/genAI) - includes a [student](https://libguides.library.arizona.edu/students-chatgpt/) and an [instructor guide](https://libguides.library.arizona.edu/ai-literacy-instructors)
