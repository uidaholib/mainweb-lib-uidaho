The University already publishes its own guidance on using AI at work.

## Start here

- [Inside U of I -- U of I AI Resources and Guidance](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/sites/InsideUI-AIatUIdaho/SitePages/Home.aspx) - The central intranet site providing guidance on using AI, including information on using AI in the classroom, administrative tasks, and research, as well as developing AI systems.
- [Guidance for Artificial Intelligence](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/3464/Guidance-for-Artificial-Intelligence) - An OIT page providing general guidance on AI use. It covers which AI services are approved for which kinds of University data, which are restricted, and what to do with the output an AI tool gives you. Read it before putting University information into any AI tool.
- [How to identify High Risk data](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/1659/How-to-identify-High-Risk-data) - OIT's explanation of how to tell which risk category your data falls into. This is the companion to the guidance above: it tells you which bucket your data is in, so you know which part applies to you.
- [AI Glossary](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/3460/AI-Glossary) - Plain-language definitions of common AI terminology.

## What's happening at U of I

- [AI @ U of I](https://www.uidaho.edu/about/thinking-big/ai-at-uidaho) - The University's public-facing AI site. Collects news and events on how AI is being used across campus.
- [OIT's Artificial Intelligence knowledge base](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Category/329/Artificial-Intelligence-AI) - Every AI article OIT publishes, gathered in one place.
- [AI Memo from OIT and the AI Execution Group](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/2823/Artificial-Intelligence-Memo-from-OIT-and-AI-Execution-Group) - The campus-wide memo setting out the University's expectations for AI use, effective Fall 2025.

## University contacts for AI questions

For questions about what AI is and how to begin experimenting with it, contact the [Data Hub]({{ '/datahub/' | relative_url }}).

For questions about whether a particular AI software or tool is safe to use, or what information you are allowed to send to an AI, contact OIT through [their support portal](https://support.uidaho.edu/).

For more advanced questions about how to build an AI tool, evaluate it, and how to ensure it complies with relevant policies and procedures, contact:

- OIT's AI Apps team at <aiapps@uidaho.edu> with general inquiries.
- The [AI for Research Administration](https://ai4ra.uidaho.edu/contact/) team with inquiries about research administration.

For questions about the University's data-sovereign AI platform, [MindRouter]({{ '/datahub/mindrouter.html' | relative_url }}), reach out to Research Computing and Data Services at <rcds@uidaho.edu>.
