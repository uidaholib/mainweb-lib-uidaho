Students can get overwhelmed in the world of information, and can benefit from library faculty teaching skills for efficiently finding and evaluating information. The U of I Library offers the a variety of instructional services for students and faculty.

English 1102 Instruction

Students in English 1102 will get a basic introduction to information research in connection with their research paper assignment. See our English 1102 Research Guide for details.

Information Literacy Instruction

Students often need additional instruction in more sophisticated information sources for particular assignments or disciplines in other courses. Librarians can lead classroom sessions to help!

Contact your library liaison.

Additional Resources

  • Instruction Calendar: find the location of ENGL 102 and other sessions.
  • Research Guides: These web pages provide recommended sources for particular courses or disciplines.
  • Library Literacies: To clarify the distinct educational value that the Library provides to our students, the Library faculty has defined learning literacies that articulate our instructional goals.
  • Course Instructor Evaluation Form
  • Research Assistance Program: faculty and students are encouraged to make an appointment with a librarian when individual research assistance is needed.
