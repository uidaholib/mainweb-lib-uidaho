In June 2026, the U of I Library, as a member of Lyrasis, entered into a [Read and Publish agreement with Wiley](https://authors.wiley.com/author-resources/Journal-Authors/open-access/affiliation-policies-payments/lyrasis-agreement.html).

## Benefits for U of I affiliates:

- Waiver of OA publishing fees for close to 1,250 hybrid journals

## Requirements:

- The U of I author must be the corresponding author of the article accepted for publication on or after January 1, 2026.
- The article must be primary research or a review article. Other article types e.g. letters, editorials etc. are excluded.
- This agreement cannot be used to cover additional charges (e.g. cover, color, and page charges), which individual journals administer separately.
- Have an article accepted in an [eligible journal](https://authors.wiley.com/asset/Wiley-Journal-APCs-OnlineOpen.xlsx).
