The Library offers a [Loanable Equipment Collection](https://alliance-uidaho.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/collectionDiscovery?vid=01ALLIANCE_UID:UID&collectionId=81376160580001851&lang=en) for patrons to use. From small items you might have forgotten like a bike lock, calculator, or charger, to larger tech like cameras, laptops, or even sewing machines, the Library has you covered!

[Browse Loanable Equipment](https://alliance-uidaho.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/collectionDiscovery?vid=01ALLIANCE_UID:UID&collectionId=81376160580001851&lang=en){:.btn .btn-pride-gold}

Check the item's listing in the catalog to see availability and location. 
Items maybe located at the main Library Circulation Desk or at the [Gary Strong Curriculum Center](/services/curriculumcenter.html).
A limited selection of equipment can be reserved in advance using the [Reservable Tech Equipment Collection](https://alliance-uidaho.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/collectionDiscovery?vid=01ALLIANCE_UID:UID&collectionId=81376160560001851&lang=en).
Please note, equipment is subject to [late fines and replacement fees]({{ '/services/borrow/#fines' | relative_url }}).

Highlights include:

- Audio: headphones, voice recorders, and microphones.
- Video: camcorders, GoPro, and green screen.
- Photography: DSLRs, tripods, and 360 degree camera.
- Laptops, iPads, and calculators.
- Meetings: presentation remote, digital projectors, meeting owl, and web cams.
- Accessories: phone and computer chargers, cables, drawing pad, external hard drives, and bike lock.

You may also want to explore equipment available for use in [The Studio A/V Lab]({{ '/studio/' | relative_url }}).
