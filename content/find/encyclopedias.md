Our reference resources &#8212; encyclopedias, dictionaries, gazetteers, glossaries, thesauruses, indexes, guide books, etc. &#8212; can help you define and understand the fundamental terms and ideas of a discipline.

<a href="https://www.wikipedia.org/">Wikipedia</a> is always a good place to start, but our reference resources can help you find more specialized information and guidelines.

<a href="https://libguides.uidaho.edu/az/databases?s=131601" class="btn btn-outline-pride-gold my-2">Start with Our Reference Databases</a>

Our print reference resources can be found on the first floor of the library, near the Circulation Desk.

Here are some popular reference resources:

- [ASAE Gateway to Associations](http://www.asaecenter.org/Directories/AssociationSearch.cfm?navItemNumber=16581)
- [Electronic Style Guides](https://libguides.uidaho.edu/citing)

- [Pivot](https://pivot.proquest.com/funding_main)
