## Contribute to the largest library in Idaho!

<a class="btn btn-lg btn-outline-pride-gold my-2" href="https://giving.uidaho.edu/campaigns/library-deans-excellence-fund-2">Make a Gift!</a>

Since 1892, the University of Idaho Library has grown from a single classroom in the University Administration Building to become the largest library in the state of Idaho, providing access to millions of resources and materials for U of I students, faculty, and alumni, as well as Idaho citizens.

<img src="{{ site.lib-media }}/carousel/LibraryClockTower_car.jpg" class="img-fluid mb-3" alt="University of Idaho Library">

We house well over a million books and thousands of print and online periodical subscriptions. We continue, as we have for over a century, to serve as an official regional depository of U.S. federal government publications, making <a href="https://libguides.uidaho.edu/Documents">almost two million government documents</a> available to the public.

--------

## Bringing Idaho to the World

Not satisfied with only bringing the world to Idaho, we also bring Idaho to the world. Our <a href="{{ '/special-collections/' | relative_url }}">Special Collections and Archives</a> provide researchers access to unique historical photographs, archival maps and documents, rare books, and the <a href="http://www.ijc.uidaho.edu/">International Jazz Collections</a>, the premier jazz archives of the Pacific Northwest.

Our <a href="https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/">Center for Digital Inquiry and Learning</a> makes many of these and other unique resources freely available worldwide through <a href="{{ '/digital/' | relative_url }}">Digital Collections</a> like the <a href="{{ '/digital/gem/' | relative_url }}">Gem of the Mountains Digital Yearbook Collection</a> and the <a href="{{ '/digital/postcards/' | relative_url }}">Northwest Historical Postcards Collection</a> — and builds the open-source software, used by libraries worldwide, that puts them there.

## We could not do this without our donors

Donors fund our fellowships, seed our grant-winning projects, build our spaces, develop our collections, and furnish our study areas.

- See <a href="why.html">what your gifts build</a> — fellowships, digital scholarship, data and GIS research, new spaces, and growing collections.
- Explore <a href="ways.html">ways to give and where your gift can go</a> — cash, planned gifts, appreciated assets, in-kind donations, and the Library's endowments.

Or simply make a gift online using <a href="https://giving.uidaho.edu/campaigns/library-deans-excellence-fund-2">U of I Giving</a> and ensure the designation "Library Dean's Excellence Fund" is selected.

We thank you for your interest and for your support of the University of Idaho Library.
