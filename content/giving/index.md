## Contribute to the largest library in Idaho!

<a class="btn btn-lg btn-outline-pride-gold my-2" href="https://giving.uidaho.edu/campaigns/library-deans-excellence-fund-2">Make a Gift!</a>

Last year the University of Idaho Library recorded more than 278,000 visits — a 10% increase over the year before. Those visitors came for a lot of different reasons. Some came for the million-plus books and the thousands of journals and databases. Some came to run a laser cutter in <a href="{{ '/mill/' | relative_url }}">the MILL</a>, record a podcast in <a href="{{ '/studio/' | relative_url }}">the Studio</a>, or get help with a map in <a href="{{ '/datahub/' | relative_url }}">the Data Hub</a>. Some came to the second floor, where research help, writing support, and tutoring now sit side by side.

<img src="{{ site.lib-media }}/carousel/LibraryClockTower_car.jpg" class="img-fluid mb-3" alt="University of Idaho Library">

Since 1892 this library has grown from a single classroom in the Administration Building into the largest in the state — and, as an official regional depository, the place where <a href="https://libguides.uidaho.edu/Documents">almost two million U.S. government documents</a> are kept available to any Idahoan who needs them.

--------

## Bringing Idaho to the World

Not satisfied with only bringing the world to Idaho, we also bring Idaho to the world. Our <a href="{{ '/special-collections/' | relative_url }}">Special Collections and Archives</a> hold historical photographs, archival maps, rare books, the papers that document this state's landscapes and politics, and the <a href="http://www.ijc.uidaho.edu/">International Jazz Collections</a>, the premier jazz archive of the Pacific Northwest — much of it found nowhere else in the world.

Our <a href="https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/">Center for Digital Inquiry and Learning</a> puts that material online through <a href="{{ '/digital/' | relative_url }}">Digital Collections</a> and student-built projects like <a href="{{ '/digital/taylor-archive/' | relative_url }}">the Taylor Wilderness Research Station Archive</a> and <a href="https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/keeping-watch/">Keeping Watch</a>. It also builds the open-source software that gets it there — tools now used by libraries, museums, and classrooms around the world.

## None of it happens without our donors

Donors fund our fellowships, seed the projects that go on to win federal grants, build our spaces, and develop our collections.

- See <a href="why.html">what your gifts build</a> — fellowships, digital scholarship, research spaces, and a growing archive.
- Explore <a href="ways.html">ways to give and where your gift can go</a> — cash, planned gifts, appreciated assets, in-kind donations, and the Library's endowments.

Or simply make a gift online through <a href="https://giving.uidaho.edu/campaigns/library-deans-excellence-fund-2">U of I Giving</a> with the designation "Library Dean's Excellence Fund" selected.
