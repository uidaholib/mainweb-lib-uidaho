[GIS Day @ U of I 2026](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/gisday/) is coming up Nov 18th, register now to join us!

GIS Day @ University of Idaho is a free event made possible by the U of I Library that brings together scholars, students, professionals, businesses, and the public to discuss geospatial technologies. This year's theme is *From Risk to Recovery: GIS and Wildfire*, exploring the ways GIS is helping us navigate increasing fire impacts across our region. Speakers include:

- Heather Heward (Forest, Rangeland and Fire Sciences), "Adult Learning Principles for GIS Instruction: Lessons Learned from Teaching Wildland Fire Professionals"
- Keith T. Weber (GIS Director, Idaho State University), "Better Understanding Wildfire with Spatial Data: Pre-fire, Post-fire, and Past-fires"
- Jason Karl (Director of the Rangeland Center, Rangeland Ecology), Ed Flathers (College of Natural Resources Forest Innovations Institute), Bruce Ripley (Chief innovation officer, College of Natural Resources), "Remotely Sensed Data in Natural Resources"

The sessions will take place on Wednesday, Nov 18th, 2026 as a hybrid event hosted at Clearwater room of the Idaho Student Union Building and streaming live via Zoom.
