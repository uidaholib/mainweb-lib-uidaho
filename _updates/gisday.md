[GIS Day @ U of I 2026](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/gisday/) is coming up Nov 18th, and the [Call for GIS Day short talks and posters](https://uidaho.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2tylxMGgvNUqsbY) is now open!

We invite submissions from across the geospatial and GIS community for ten minute presentations or research posters highlighting your recent geospatial-related work, project, organization, or research. Please [submit using our form](https://uidaho.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2tylxMGgvNUqsbY) by Oct 23rd.

GIS Day @ University of Idaho is a free event made possible by the U of I Library that brings together scholars, students, professionals, businesses, and the public to discuss geospatial technologies and demonstrate their many uses. This year's theme is *From Risk to Recovery: GIS and Wildfire*, exploring the ways GIS is helping us navigate increasing fire impacts across our region.

The sessions will take place on Wednesday, Nov 18th, 2026 as a hybrid event hosted at Clearwater room of the Idaho Student Union Building and streaming live via Zoom.
