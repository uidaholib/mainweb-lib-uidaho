Library Special Collections and Archives has created a [guide for Idaho Downinders](https://libguides.uidaho.edu/c.php?g=1560115&p=11708177) looking to file Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (RECA) claims. 
As Spec has seen overwhelming requests for assistance, the guide brings together curated resources and information to help navigate the RECA process, the required documentation, and Library services.

Please visit the [Idaho Downwinders RECA Guide](https://libguides.uidaho.edu/c.php?g=1560115&p=11708177) to learn more.
